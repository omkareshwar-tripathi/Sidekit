Before you start.
1. Relaunch the fresh build: open ~/SpeakType/SpeakTypeMac/SpeakType.app
2. You need a working camera (built-in FaceTime cam is fine). The Mirror strip lives inside the Shelf panel — open the Shelf from the menu-bar icon first.
3. Watch the green camera light on every test — the core privacy invariant is "camera runs only while the Mirror is open (not collapsed), and the light must go OFF on every exit." Treat a stuck-on light as a FAIL.
4. The edge-light tests read best in a dim room (you should see your face brighten).
5. For the permission test you ideally want a fresh camera-permission state — if you've already granted it, you can still walk the rest; to re-test the prompt, revoke under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
6. Tick the box when walked, mark pass/fail, add notes. State persists in this browser. Hit ⤓ Export at the bottom and paste the results back to Claude.
